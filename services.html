Massage Therapy Services

Massage therapy that combines a relaxing experience with focused attention to your treatment goals.

A personal approach

Care shaped
around you

Whether you’re coming in to unwind, address a particular area of discomfort, or work toward more comfortable movement, your appointment is shaped around your needs.

My approach combines flowing massage with focused work where appropriate, while keeping your comfort and preferences central to the treatment. We’ll discuss what you’d like to focus on and adjust the pressure and approach together.

A range of techniques, one tailored appointment

Treatment may draw on Swedish massage, deeper pressure, myofascial techniques, trigger point work and gentle joint mobilization. These approaches can be combined within your appointment—you don’t need to choose a technique before booking.

Comfort, communication & care

Your appointment

We’ll begin with a brief conversation about how you’re feeling and what you’d like from the session. Assessment helps guide the treatment, and we can discuss movement or home-care suggestions when useful.

Your feedback is welcome throughout the appointment, including any changes you’d like to pressure, positioning or areas of focus.
