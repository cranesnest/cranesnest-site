The person behind Crane’s Nest

Meet Stephen Crane, RMT

I’m Stephen Crane, a Registered Massage Therapist based in Parksville. I enjoy working with my hands, meeting people and providing care that responds to each person’s needs.

A personal approach to care

My approach combines flowing, relaxing massage with focused attention to the areas that brought you in. Your goals guide the treatment, and I adapt the pressure and pace to your comfort and preferences. I want your appointment to feel welcoming, unhurried and centred on you.

My path to massage therapy

Massage therapy is my second career, bringing together my enjoyment of practical, hands-on work and helping others. I graduated from the West Coast College of Massage Therapy in Victoria in 2024.

I’ve lived in British Columbia throughout my life, including North Vancouver, Victoria and the Fraser Valley, and have called Parksville home since 2022.
