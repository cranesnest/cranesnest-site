What to know before your appointment

Payment

All major payment methods are accepted. Any balance not covered by insurance is payable at the appointment.

Insurance & direct billing

Registered Massage Therapy may be eligible for extended-health benefits, depending on your plan. Direct billing is available for many insurers, subject to your coverage and any required authorization.

You are responsible for any amount your plan does not cover. For participating insurers, what to bring and details about Medavie Blue Cross, see insurance and direct-billing information.

Common pricing questions

Are the prices the same for a first appointment?

Yes. Initial and subsequent visits have the same rates, and new patients can book any of the listed durations.

Is GST included?

No. The listed prices are before GST, which is added at checkout.

Do I need a referral?

You do not need a referral to book. Check your insurance plan for any referral requirements for reimbursement.

What if I need to cancel?

Please provide at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations and missed appointments may incur a fee of up to the full appointment price, at the practitioner’s discretion. Read the cancellation policy.
